About Me

My name is Tushaar Bansal. I am an international student from India and a Management Information Systems student at San Diego State University.

Over time, I became especially interested in artificial intelligence through research and hands-on projects. Working on AI-related ideas helped me better understand how technology can be applied to real-world problems.

I enjoy learning about intelligent systems, research, and practical technology. My academic background in MIS and my growing interest in AI continue to shape the kind of work I want to do.

This website is a place where I share some of my writing, interests, and work.
